## Research rules

- Use the vendor's official pricing or support page whenever possible.
- Record the exact country and charged currency. Do not convert a price.
- Keep unsupported or unverified country coverage absent instead of guessing.
- Close the previous `priceHistory` window before recording a confirmed change.

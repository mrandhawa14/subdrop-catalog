DueLume

Privacy Policy

Effective August 28, 2026

DueLume is designed so the developer does not collect your personal data. There is no account with DueLume, no advertising SDK, and no analytics or cross-app tracking.

Your subscription data

The subscriptions, prices, notes, categories, lists, budgets, and settings you enter are stored on your device. When iCloud is available, Apple’s CloudKit may sync that data between your Apple devices or with people you explicitly invite to a shared list. The developer cannot access your private iCloud database.

Network requests

DueLume downloads a public reference catalog, exchange rates, tax rates, and service artwork so it can provide current reference information. These requests do not contain the subscription data you entered, advertising identifiers, or a DueLume account identifier. The hosting provider may process standard technical request information such as an IP address under its own policies.

Notifications and purchases

Renewal reminders are scheduled using Apple system services. In-app purchases are processed by Apple; DueLume receives only the purchase entitlement needed to unlock Pro. Apple’s handling of this information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Exporting and deleting data

You choose when to export or share a calendar or CSV file. You can delete individual subscriptions in the app. Removing the app and its iCloud data through Apple’s settings removes the remaining app data under Apple’s retention processes.

Children

DueLume is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Questions can be sent through the DueLume support page.
